About

Fire behaves differently in space

On Earth, hot gas rises and a flame pulls into a pointed cone. In microgravity there is no buoyancy, so flames burn as oval balls, and flame spread and smoke move in ways ground tests do not show.

On a mission far from Earth a fire can be catastrophic, so how materials burn and how smoke spreads in microgravity is central to spacecraft fire safety. NASA has studied it in combustion experiments aboard the ISS and the Cygnus spacecraft.

FlameIntel turns NASA PSI combustion research into a searchable, explainable, data-aware dashboard for future space habitats.

How it works

From NASA files to cited answers

  1. 1

    Collect

    NASA PSI files for 24 experiments.

  2. 2

    Read

    PDF text, Excel rows and table summaries.

  3. 3

    Chunk

    13,112 searchable passages, each tagged with experiment, document and page.

  4. 4

    Index

    Embeddings with BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5, stored in ChromaDB.

  5. 5

    Answer

    The 8 closest passages go to the AI, which answers only from them, with citations.

Built to be trusted

  • Every claim cited

    Each factual sentence ends with a source tag you can open: experiment, document and page.

  • Says “Not in the data”

    When the sources do not cover a question, the assistant says so instead of guessing.

  • No predictions

    No forecasts, safety probabilities or certifications. Only what the sources state.

  • Numbers checked in code

    A program compares every number in an answer with the cited passages and flags any it cannot find.

Limits

What we do not claim

FlameIntel organizes existing research. It does not predict, certify equipment or replace NASA sources. And the data has gaps:

  • 237 files were skipped when indexing: 159 raw lab sheets or plots and 78 PDFs with no extractable text. Their content is not searchable.
  • 9 downloads failed, so those documents are missing.
  • FLEX and PSI-20 keep most of their data in tables, so the text index describes them less deeply.
  • SAFFIRE-I, II and III have very similar text. Check the experiment on every citation.
  • The free AI quota is limited, so answers can be slow or switch to a backup model.
